OGGI Free CRM — clickable prototype

Every screen in SCREENMAP.md, tappable. This is a drawing, not software: nothing is saved and no data is real. It exists so the shape of the product can be argued with before a line of it is built.

11 August 2026 · v1 scope · screen-login

What to look at

  • Add contact — the duplicate warning. It warns and offers to open the existing record. It never blocks. Hard-blocking teaches users to leave the phone field empty.
  • Contact — the WhatsApp window countdown. Meta rejects free-form messages once the 24-hour window closes, so the composer has to change before you type, not after you send.
  • Gone quiet — what replaces recurring tasks. It only fires when the relationship actually went cold, and clears itself when you make contact.
  • Recycle bin — reachable from Contacts, not buried in Settings. Undo you cannot find is undo that does not exist.
  • Home, empty — the first-ever screen. Most CRM trials die here.

Why a prototype before code

Of ten real failures in previous OGGI builds, one came from bad planning and eight from nobody checking the live product. This prototype is not there to make planning longer — it is there so that the thing being checked later is a thing that was agreed earlier.

Every screen here maps to a feature file in features/ and a row in docs/FEATURE-MATRIX.csv. If a screen exists here and nowhere in that matrix, the completeness check fails the build.
